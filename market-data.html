Data Center Prices: Colocation, Power & Build Costs — Market Data

Data center pricing has flipped from a buyer's market to a landlord's market, and the numbers now read like a vacancy report with rent attached. National vacancy sits near historic lows while asking rents climb for a fourth straight year — a combination that leaves tenants paying more for space that is harder to find.

The figures below separate asking rents from signed economics. Asking rents are what landlords publish for a 250–500 kW requirement; build costs and power prices explain why those rents keep rising even as construction hits records. Treat any single per-kW figure as a market quote, not a quote for your requirement.

Methodology

Included: CBRE published asking rents and vacancy figures (as reported in CBRE releases and trade-press summaries of CBRE reports), JLL build-cost averages, and utility/operator power prices as compiled in public benchmarks. Excluded: negotiated signed rents, which operators do not publish, and any broker's private comp set. Asking rents vary by requirement size — the requirement size is stated on every rent figure — so figures from different requirement tiers are not averaged together.

Trend labels: “Baseline (October 2026) — trend tracking starts with the next refresh” means no prior-period figure from the same source was available this session, so no movement is claimed. Where a prior figure is shown, the trend compares the current figure with that prior figure from the same source.
